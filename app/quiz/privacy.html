Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how our Science Quiz Game (the "App") handles information. We do not collect, use, or share any personal data from our users. This policy is designed to be transparent with parents, guardians, and children about our data practices.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Our App does not collect or store any of the following:

  • Personal information (name, email address, phone number, mailing address)
  • Account login credentials or registration information
  • Location data or GPS information
  • Photos, videos, or audio recordings
  • Contact lists or friend lists
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, MAC address, or Advertising ID)
  • Behavioral tracking or activity logs
  • Quiz answers or game progress

3. How the App Works

The Science Quiz Game functions entirely offline or within your device with no connection to external servers to collect user data. All gameplay, quiz questions, scores, and progress are stored locally on your device only and are not transmitted to any third-party servers.

4. Device Permissions

If the App requests any device permissions, they are used solely for the stated functionality and not for data collection purposes. Examples include:

  • Internet Access: If requested, only for loading initial resources and not for tracking users
  • Storage Access: Only to store game data and settings locally on your device

5. Third-Party Services

Our App does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising networks, or data collection services. We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase, or similar tracking services.

6. COPPA Compliance

This App is designed with children under 13 in mind and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Since we do not collect any personal information from children, parental consent is not required.

7. Google Play Store Compliance

This App complies with Google Play Store policies regarding children's apps and data privacy. We do not request sensitive permissions unnecessarily and do not transmit personal data.

8. Security

Since no personal data is collected, there is no personal data to protect or lose. Game progress and settings are stored only on the local device and remain under the user's control.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy
